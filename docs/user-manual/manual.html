Chapter 1

What the app does

Quick-commerce platforms (Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart and others) send Nootie purchase orders (POs). Each PO must be accepted, picked, packed, dispatched, delivered and finally confirmed by the platform with a GRN (goods received note) before Nootie is paid. The PO Control Tower follows every PO through those steps and points out the ones that are stuck.

What you get

  • One list of every PO from every platform, built automatically from the Warehouse Master sheet and the platform PO files.
  • A stage for every PO (decision pending, in warehouse, in transit, GRN pending, closed…), so anyone can see where it is.
  • Problem flags with an owner team and a next action, such as "Expiring soon → prioritise dispatch and book the appointment now".
  • A control tower per platform, plus an overall one, with a worklist, handover check, daily reconciliation and issue log.
  • A dashboard with charts: activity over time, platform mix, lead time, ageing and an expiry timeline.
  • Imports for every document the team already produces: invoices (including Vasy PDFs), GRNs, the daily dispatch report, PRN and DN files.

Chapter 2

How everything connects

Data comes in from Google Sheets and uploaded files. The app joins it into one record per PO, works out each PO's stage and flags, and shows the result on the dashboard and control towers.

Three rules worth remembering

  1. The PO number is the key. Every file and sheet is matched to a PO by its PO number. If a number is typed differently (extra spaces, a missing prefix), the app treats it as a different PO.
  2. The Warehouse Master creates POs; the other files fill them in. The sync adds every PO number it finds. Platform PO files then add the SKU lines, and invoice, GRN and tracker updates add dispatch and receipt details.
  3. The app never overwrites what the team decided. A decision, handover or acknowledgement recorded in the app always wins over what a sheet suggests.

Chapter 4

The life of a PO

Every PO sits in exactly one of nine stages. Stages 1 to 4 are "open" (nothing dispatched yet). Stages 5 to 8 start once there is an invoice. Stage 9 means the PO will never be fulfilled.

Stage by stage

#StageThe PO is here when…Next step
1Received, decision pendingNo invoice, and no decision yet (Pending).Order desk accepts or declines
2Accepted, handover pendingAccepted, but the printout has not been handed to the warehouse.Hand over the printout
3Handed to warehouse, ack pendingHandover date recorded, no acknowledgement yet.Warehouse acknowledges
4In warehouse, pending dispatchWarehouse acknowledged; no invoice yet.Dispatch and invoice
5In transitAt least one invoice is Dispatched, In Transit or Arrived.Track to delivery
6Delivered, GRN pendingNothing in transit, but at least one invoice has GRN Pending.Chase GRN
7RTO / returnedAn invoice came back (RTO) and none was delivered.Credit note / re-dispatch
8Closed, delivered and GRN doneInvoiced and nothing above applies.Done
9Closed, not fulfilledNo invoice, and the decision is Declined, Cancelled or Expired.Done (reason recorded)
A PO with several invoices (a split shipment) takes the stage of its "least finished" invoice: if one box is still in transit, the PO is in transit.
Chapter 5

Who does what, every day

Each team owns certain flags. Start the day on the Worklist, filtered to your team's flags, and clear them top to bottom.

Order desk / SCM: morning

  1. Open Control tower → Worklist.
  2. Clear 1 Expired and 2 Expiring soon first.
  3. Decide every 7 No decision PO; pick a reason when declining.
  4. Hand printouts to the warehouse and record it in Handover check.
  5. Once a week, close out 10 Unaccounted, legacy.

Warehouse: through the day

  1. Acknowledge every printout you received (Handover check → Acknowledge).
  2. Work 5 In warehouse, not dispatched, oldest first.
  3. Make sure every dispatch has an invoice in the Tracker sheet or the invoice upload.

Logistics

  1. Chase 6 In transit overdue.
  2. Keep status, AWB and delivery date current in the Tracker sheet.

Accounts

  1. Chase 8 GRN pending; raise 9 Credit note pending.
End of day: open Daily recon to compare what was dispatched today against what was planned, and log anything odd as an Issue.

Opening a PO: the journey drawer

Click any PO number in the control tower and a panel slides in from the right. It shows the PO's stage and flag, the next action, a dot for each step of the journey (green = done, red = overdue, grey = not yet), and the Team fields you can edit.

Recording progress on one PO

  1. Open the PO from All POs or the Worklist.
  2. Under Team fields, set the Decision (and a Reason if declining), or fill Handed to warehouse on + Handed to (person), or Warehouse ack on + ack by.
  3. Click Save team fields. The stage and flag update straight away, and the change is written to the audit log.

Chapter 8

The logic behind the numbers

This chapter is the rulebook. Everything on the dashboard and in the control tower comes from these rules.

8.1 How a flag is chosen

A PO gets at most one flag: the first rule below that matches, in this order. Flags 1 to 6 count as urgent (the red badges in the sidebar).

#FlagTeamWhat to do
1Expired, not dispatchedOrder deskDispatch now or ask the platform for an expiry extension.
2Expiring soonOrder deskPrioritise dispatch and book the appointment now.
3Printout not handed to warehouseOrder deskFind the printout, hand it to the warehouse, log the date.
4Warehouse has not acknowledgedWarehouseAcknowledge receipt of the PO printout.
5In warehouse, not dispatchedWarehousePick, pack and dispatch; log the invoice.
6In transit overdueLogisticsChase the courier and update the delivery status.
7No decisionOrder deskDecide Accept or Decline and record the reason.
8GRN pendingAccountsChase the GRN from the platform.
9Credit note pendingAccountsRaise or chase the credit note.
10Unaccounted, legacyOrder deskBacklog: confirm whether it was declined, expired or lost, then set the decision.

8.3 How the decision is worked out

The order desk's decision in the app always wins. Until someone records one, the app infers it:

  • The PO has an invoice → Accepted.
  • The Warehouse Master says not fulfilled or cancelled → Declined, Cancelled or Expired (with the sheet's reason).
  • An appointment is booked → Accepted.
  • Otherwise → Pending.

8.4 Where invoices come from

A PO can have several invoices (split shipments). The app collects them in this order and never counts the same invoice twice:

  1. The latest Dispatch & GRN Tracker sync (one row per invoice, with status, GRN status, shortages, damages and credit notes).
  2. Invoices from invoice uploads that are not on the Tracker.
  3. The PO's dispatch record.

8.5 The journey and its gaps

The journey has eight steps: PO received → Accepted → Printout handed → Warehouse acknowledged → Invoiced → Dispatched → Delivered → GRN done. A later step implies the earlier ones (an invoiced PO counts as accepted and handed over even if nobody recorded it). The gaps count POs that stopped between two steps; POs that were never going to be fulfilled (stage 9) are left out of the gaps.

8.6 Platform detection

When a file has no platform column, or someone picks the wrong platform on upload, the app recognises the platform from the PO number:

PO number looks likePlatformExample
P followed by 6+ digitsZeptoP7654321
Letters + "PO" + digitsSwiggy InstamartDELPO123456
Starts with FLSFlipkartFLS998877
12 to 14 digits onlyBlinkit123456789012
Starts with IRABigBasketIRA1234
Starts with PUR-ORDSupertailsPUR-ORD-5512
"eTrade" in the platform columnAmazonn/a

Order of trust: the file's own platform column → the PO-number pattern → the platform you chose when uploading.

8.7 Fill rate and value

  • PO value = the PO amount; if the PO has no amount, the invoiced value is used.
  • Fill rate = invoiced value ÷ PO value (only for POs that have an amount).
Chapter 9

Getting data in

Most data arrives on its own through the sheet syncs. Uploads cover the documents that don't live in a sheet.

9.1 Warehouse Master: the starting point

The Warehouse Master is where every PO number first appears. The app reads its ORDER BOOK, DISPATCH + GRN, GRN, Blinkit, Flipkart, Zepto and Swiggy tabs.

Setting up the automatic sync (once)

  1. In the app, open Uploads → Warehouse Master and copy the script shown there.
  2. Open the Warehouse Master Google Sheet → Extensions → Apps Script. Delete any code, paste the script.
  3. Replace PASTE_THE_SYNC_KEY_HERE with the sync key (Railway → po-control-tower-backend → Variables → SHEET_SYNC_KEY). Save.
  4. Choose the function setup and click Run. Allow the permissions Google asks for. This runs a first sync and repeats it every 15 minutes.
  5. Back in the app, the Last sync or import box shows the result: PO numbers found, created, already in the app, invoice rows, and a split by platform and decision.
Edit access needed. Only someone who can edit the Warehouse Master can add the script. If you can only view it, ask the sheet's owner to do these steps or to give you edit access.

Manual alternative

Download the sheet as Excel (File → Download → Microsoft Excel) and upload it on the same page with Import Warehouse Master. Uploading again only adds new PO numbers.

9.2 The other uploads

UploadWhat to uploadWhat it changes
PO filesThe platform's PO export (Excel/CSV), one platform per file or mixed.Creates POs and their SKU lines; adds lines to POs that already exist. Platform detected per row.
InvoicesAn invoice register (Excel/CSV) or a Vasy PDF export.Matches each invoice to its PO (by PO number, else invoice number) and records invoice no., date, value. Vasy PDFs: Blinkit invoices only; also sets dispatched quantity per SKU.

How every upload behaves

  1. Choose the file and click upload. The app replies straight away and works in the background, so you can leave the page.
  2. A progress bar shows rows done, matched and failed. Large files take a few minutes.
  3. When it finishes, open the batch to see each row's result: matched, PO not found, skipped (for example a non-Blinkit invoice in a Vasy PDF) or an error message.

9.3 Google Sheets sync (Tracker and ALL PO DATA)

This page connects the Master Dispatch & GRN Tracker to the app with its own Apps Script (same sync key). It updates:

  • Dispatch: dispatch date, invoice number and value, docket/AWB, delivery partner, status, comment.
  • Delivery: appointment date, delivery date and PO expiry date.
  • GRN: when GRN Status is Completed: shortage, damage, excess, discrepancy amount and credit note number.

The optional ALL PO DATA tab gets every PO's current state from the app. Columns with the same names as the Tracker can be edited in the sheet and flow back to the app; app-calculated columns (PO status, fill rate, GRN outcome…) are refreshed on every sync and edits to them are ignored.

Chapter 13

Admin and settings

Settings

  • Control Tower SLA days: the nine settings in section 8.2. Changing them re-flags POs within a minute.
  • Risk thresholds: the days-to-expiry bands used to colour risk on PO screens (defaults 1 / 2 / 4 / 7). They must be whole numbers in ascending order.

Only admins can save settings; every change goes into the audit log.

Masters, locations and users

  • Masters: customers, transporters and owners used in dropdowns (delivery partner list in the sheet comes from here).
  • Locations & TAT: platform warehouses and the transit time to each.
  • Users: add people, set their role, reset passwords, deactivate leavers.
